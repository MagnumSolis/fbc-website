How validated is your idea?

Be honest. Nothing is saved unless you're signed in.

01I can describe the problem in one sentence, without mentioning my solution.
02I know exactly who has this problem: a specific group, not “everyone”.
03I've talked to at least 10 people who have this problem.
04They're actively trying to solve it today, with workarounds, money or time.
05I know the existing alternatives and why they fall short.
06Someone has said they'd pay, pre-order, or sign up for this.
07I know why we're the right people to build this, and why now.
